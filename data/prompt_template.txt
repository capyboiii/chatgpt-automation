Use the design concept described above as the core creative direction and apply it to every uploaded transparent PNG product mockup.

RULES:
- Treat all uploaded mockups in the same request as one product collection generated in a single run.
- Automatically identify each product type, material, printable area, perspective, folds, curvature, lighting, and orientation.
- Preserve the original product form, silhouette, proportions, camera angle, folds, material, and overall structure of each uploaded mockup.
- Keep the core design concept consistent across all products, including the main theme, message, style, niche, and key visual identity.
- You may adapt the design layout, composition, scale, placement, arrangement, and colors so it fits each product form in the most visually appealing and commercially effective way.
- Improve contrast, hierarchy, balance, and readability where needed.
- Do not change the design into a different concept. The original niche, message, and creative direction must remain recognizable.
- Make the design look realistically printed or applied to the actual product material, following perspective, folds, curvature, texture, lighting, shadows, and reflections. Never make it look like a flat pasted sticker.

BACKGROUND — VERY IMPORTANT:
- The background for each product must be derived directly from the design niche and design concept.
- First analyze the design and identify:
  1. niche
  2. sub-niche
  3. subject matter
  4. target audience
  5. lifestyle or culture connected to the niche
  6. mood and emotion
  7. era or aesthetic
  8. key objects, environments, activities, symbols, and visual references associated with that niche
- Then create a background environment that clearly belongs to that exact niche.

BACKGROUND VARIATION RULE:
- If multiple mockups are uploaded, each final image must have a DIFFERENT background scene.
- Do not reuse the same background composition, same environment, same prop arrangement, or same scene structure across products.
- Each product must have its own unique setting, unique composition, and unique environmental details.
- The backgrounds must clearly feel different from one another, not just slightly adjusted versions of the same scene.
- Vary the environment using different locations, angles, props, surfaces, staging, depth, and contextual details.
- Even when staying inside the same niche, create distinct scene concepts for each product.
- Avoid repetitive outputs such as the same room, same table, same wall, same landscape, or same setup with only minor changes.

CONSISTENCY ACROSS THE SET:
- All images must still feel like they belong to the same niche world, same collection, and same campaign.
- Keep consistency in overall niche identity, mood, visual quality, and art direction.
- The set should feel cohesive, but each individual background must still be clearly unique.

BACKGROUND QUALITY:
- The background should make the niche immediately understandable even without reading the design.
- Do not use a generic studio, random room, plain abstract backdrop, or unrelated setting unless the niche specifically calls for it.
- Use niche-specific environments, props, atmosphere, textures, scenery, architecture, lighting, and contextual details.
- Keep the product as the visual hero.
- Background should support the product without overpowering it.
- Use realistic depth, contact shadows, environmental reflections, and believable product integration.
- Do not add unnecessary duplicate products.

OUTPUT:
- Generate all results in one run.
- For each uploaded mockup, create exactly ONE separate finished product image.
- 1 mockup = 1 image
- 3 mockups = 3 separate images
- 5 mockups = 5 separate images
- Never combine multiple mockups into one image.
- Never create a collage, grid, contact sheet, or multi-product composition.

Every final image must:
- be square 1:1
- contain only its corresponding uploaded mockup
- use the same core design concept
- have a unique niche-appropriate background
- look realistic, attractive, and commercially polished
- be suitable for e-commerce, advertising, and social media product marketing
